## 2. Service description — factual draft

CareerScope is a candidate-controlled job-discovery and application-tracking
workspace. Its implemented workspace supports profile/preferences, authorized
job discovery, deterministic job/resume matching, saved jobs, resume handling,
and candidate-managed application tracking. A job's external application link
opens a third-party destination; opening that link does not submit an application
or change the candidate's application status.

Availability of a source or capability depends on its current permission
configuration. Unknown job, employer, verification, salary, and application
information is not represented as verified. CareerScope does not guarantee a
job, interview, offer, or employment outcome. AI and integrations must not be
described as active unless their actual release configuration and approved
scope support that claim.

## 3. Accounts and access — approval required

The repository implements email verification codes and opaque server-side
sessions. Their availability in a deployed environment has not been verified.
Guarded self-service OTP registration and atomic acceptance recording are also
implemented in the repository, but registration is disabled by default and no
acceptance is collected under this draft. Test-account selection is restricted
to explicitly configured non-production environments and is not proof of
identity or email ownership.

Counsel and the owner must decide the applicable eligibility/age language,
account-suspension and termination rules, support process, and the account
recovery commitments before this section can become operative.

## 4. Candidate-provided content — counsel completion required

Candidates may provide profile information, preferences, resumes, saved-job
details, notes, and application-tracking information. The final agreement must
define the limited purposes and permissions needed to provide those features,
address candidate responsibility for submitted material, and explain deletion,
export, and retention behavior without granting rights broader than the
implemented service requires.

The exact data-use, license, retention, export, deletion, and account-closure
terms remain for counsel review. Do not infer that a public job posting grants
CareerScope rights to collect, transform, display, redistribute, or process it
with a third party.

## 5. External destinations and providers — factual draft

Job-source providers and employer/ATS destinations are independent services.
Their terms and privacy practices may differ. CareerScope does not collect
employer credentials or submit applications on a candidate's behalf. The
repository's authentication-email adapter sends through Resend only; sender
domain verification, provider tracking settings, inbox delivery, production
configuration, and any fallback provider remain unverified or unconfigured.
